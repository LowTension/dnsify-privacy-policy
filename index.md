---

### VPN Connection

DNS Vault creates a **local VPN interface** to redirect your device's DNS traffic locally and apply your selected DNS server addresses.

**Important:** This is **not** a connection to an external endpoint or proxy server. No traffic is routed through our servers. The VPN tunnel exists entirely on your device for the sole purpose of changing your DNS configuration.
